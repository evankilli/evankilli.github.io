Not long after starting to see my partner, I was asked to join a trip to Mazatlán, Mexico to watch the upcoming April, 2024 solar eclipse along with some other friends. We ended up going just the two of us, but I wasn't complaining too much. All in all, I spent a week in the country: a few days wandering along Puerto Vallarta and its beautiful neighborhoods, a very long bus ride through the countryside, and a few more days exploring Mazatlán, a major domestic tourist destination and fishing port along Mexico's Pacific Coast. I will say, if you ever have the chance to see a solar eclipse yourself, **do it**.
