We didn't go under the best of circumstances, the whole family flew in for a funeral, but I adored my visit to Black Mountain, NC, in September, 2023. We tried our best to keep in good spirits, adventuring around the town with our younger cousins and generally staying busy. It's a trip filled with lots of memories - good and bad - and lots of emotions.
